How it works

Four steps.
Zero friction.

LiveMind runs entirely in the browser and on your own hardware. There is nothing to install for the audience.

01

Someone speaks

Audio is captured in the operator's browser. No server-side microphone. No special hardware required.

02

Speech is transcribed

A local speech-to-text engine converts audio in real time. Partial transcripts appear within milliseconds.

03

AI extracts concepts

Every 20 seconds, new transcript is sent to a local LLM. It identifies key concepts and the relationships between them.

04

The map comes alive

An animated force graph builds in real time on the audience display. Ideas grow, connect, and fade as the conversation evolves.

Built for the room

Designed to disappear
into the conversation

LiveMind is an audience experience first. Everything is tuned so the technology stays invisible — the ideas stay visible.

Self-managing graph

Maximum 24 active nodes. Concepts score on recency, frequency, and centrality. Old ideas decay after 12 minutes. No manual cleanup, ever.

Session memory

Everything is saved — transcripts, graph snapshots, node interactions. Reconnect and restore from exactly where you left off.

Separate operator view

The technician's monitor runs on a separate device — audio controls, gain meter, STT/LLM status, model switching — invisible to the audience.

Runs on your hardware

Speech transcription and LLM inference run on-host. Audio never leaves your infrastructure. No cloud dependency for core operation.

Interactive concepts

Right-click any node to pin it, rename it, merge duplicates, or remove noise. The facilitator can shape the map live without the audience noticing.

Multi-language support

99-language transcription via faster-whisper. Specialised models for European languages deliver best-in-class accuracy for local deployments.

Workshops & facilitation

Make group thinking visible

Project the map on a screen as your workshop runs. Participants see their ideas take shape in real time — it drives more focused, energised discussion and gives the facilitator a live picture of where the group's attention actually is.

Executive meetings

A living record of what was decided

Not minutes. Not a transcript. A graph of the concepts that drove the conversation, the decisions that crystallised, and the threads that were left open — generated automatically, reviewed after.

Conferences & keynotes

Turn a talk into a shared map

Display a live concept map alongside a speaker. The audience watches ideas build in real time — an ambient visual layer that reinforces key themes without competing for attention.

Research & discovery

Surface connections you didn't plan

The graph's importance scoring reveals which concepts are genuinely central versus which only felt important in the moment. Use it to drive focus in research interviews and user discovery sessions.

After the session

The conversation keeps
working after you leave

Every session is archived. The AI goes back over it and surfaces what mattered — and what you might have missed.

AI Recap

Elevator pitch, three things to retain, non-obvious connections, contradictions surfaced, decisions made, open threads — generated automatically from the session transcript.

Clean Transcript

AI-cleaned version of the raw transcription, with timestamps, ready to share or archive without the noise that comes with live speech-to-text.

Cross-Session Synthesis

Select multiple sessions from a series and synthesise the connections between them. Identify recurring themes, track how thinking evolved, surface contradictions across sessions.

Export

PDF and Markdown export for recaps and transcripts. The graph snapshot is included — a visual artefact of the conversation you can share with stakeholders who weren't in the room.
